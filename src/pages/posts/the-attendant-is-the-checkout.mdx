Queues, part 3. [Part 1](/posts/look-at-the-belt/) asked which lane to join, and [part 2](/posts/the-express-lane-is-a-fence/) asked whether to have an express lane. This one is about self-checkout.

Picture a self-checkout bank has six kiosks and one attendant. When it's slow, the obvious fix is more kiosks. In a simulation, that's almost always the wrong lever.

## The setup

One snake queue feeds a bank of kiosks. Kiosks are about 1.5× slower per item than a staffed till, because you're the one scanning. With probability *p*, a customer needs the attendant (age check, unexpected item in the bagging area, a barcode that won't scan). The attendant takes about 40 seconds per fix.

The detail that matters: **while you wait for the attendant, you keep your kiosk.** You can't step aside. The kiosk sits there, occupied and doing nothing.

## The bank holds up, then collapses

Compare six kiosks plus one attendant against three staffed tills (mean wait about 75 s):

| p (needs help) | Mean wait, 6 kiosks + 1 attendant |
|---|---|
| ≤ 0.5 | ~69 s, beats the tills |
| 0.6 | 218 s |
| 0.7 | ~6 800 s |

Up to 2.8 of the 6 kiosks were parked, each holding a customer who was waiting for help. The attendant's queue doesn't show up as a queue. It shows up as kiosks taken out of service.

## Which lever works

At p = 0.7 the system is so close to breaking that runs vary a lot. In this run the baseline wait was about 9 000 s:

- **+1 kiosk:** 1 450 s
- **Attendant 25% faster:** 103 s
- **A second attendant:** 31 s

Making the attendant faster beats adding a kiosk by about 14×. Once p goes past about 0.5, five kiosks with two attendants beats seven kiosks with one.

## When the attendant has to walk

Real attendants walk between kiosks, so a bigger bank makes each fix slower. Model a fix as 30 + 2.5·K seconds for K kiosks. Then, from p ≈ 0.5 up, there's a best size (seven kiosks at 0.5, six at 0.6). **Past it, adding kiosks makes the wait longer.**

## The rule

Effective kiosk service time is S + p·(attendant wait + fix time). The attendant's queue leaks into the kiosk bank as lost capacity. So cut *p* (better scanners, fewer age-restricted surprises) or make the attendant faster. Hardware is the wrong thing to buy.
